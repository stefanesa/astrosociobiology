title: "Field Note: Planetary Sieges"
date: 2025-04-28
type: Field Note
abstract: "A short speculative note on conquest without faster-than-light travel: why invasion is logistically hopeless, and how a patient, long-lived aggressor might take a world by slowly wrecking it."
keywords: [warfare, interstellar travel, conquest]
---

If there is no FTL travel or communication, conquering another occupied Goldilocks planet is likely impossible. You probably can't bring more materials to the system to conquer them than they can use against you from the planet.

But if you overcome sub-light travel times by being long-lived, you can probably slowly wreck a planet as an easy way to conquer the locals.

If you hit a world with a handful of large asteroids you would likely cause a global winter which, in 25 to 100 years, would kill off a large portion of the locals, destroy their manufacturing and agricultural base, and wreck their more level-headed government structures.

You could then sweep in, ally with the weakest survivors in exchange for their local knowledge (and manual labor), and take the planet as a savior (instead of as a conqueror). All you need to do then is set about causing global warming, solve it the same way you did on your homeworld, and boom: you have a new planet!
