## Assumptions

Aliens are subject to the same laws of evolution as us. No amount of space exploration will give definitive proof that any deity exists, nor that one particular religion is "right." Especially: even if aliens shoehorned some existing religion into something that could be proven "right" with sufficient exploration, that religion would either (a) not match anything on Earth, or (b) the zealots in charge of the alien branch of that religion would not recognize the liturgical relevance of any fellow travellers on Earth.

If we meet aliens and they seem religious, we should get ready for atrocities to happen.

## Why would anyone come?

If aliens have similar economics to humans, they probably similarly lack an economic rationale for interspace travel. Similarly, conquest for wealth probably doesn't make economic sense (it's easier to strip-mine an asteroid than to commit genocide on Ewoks, let alone the PITA it would be to enslave them).

So if we assume our intelligent aliens are driven by some set of rational motives, and they show up, then they're either (a) looking to expand their knowledge or (b) looking to expand their religion or follow some holy quest.

## Curiosity versus faith

If we can assume alien religion works like human religion, travelling the universe to learn potentially inconvenient facts would not be a highly funded endeavor, and it's pretty unlikely that we'd be visited by such religious fellows. I do, however, know 200 secularists who would jump on a spaceship tomorrow for 200 years if it meant they could study the rocks floating around Alpha Centauri for the rest of their life... and way more who would sacrifice seeing any of their friends or family for a chance to study alien Shakespeare, Mozart... or even Avril Lavigne.

Religion is, however, one thing that could get a spaceship full of people to leave everything behind to "bring glory to Alien God" or "convert the heathens" or "fulfill our destiny by returning to the blue planet." In some of those scenarios they might end up being harmless pilgrims, but to me the most reasonable way we meet mean, interesting aliens is if they are full of faith.
