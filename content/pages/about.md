**Astro-Sociobiology** is an open, informal journal of speculative science. Its subject is the question: *if humanity meets advanced alien life, what might that life be like?*

## Scope

Two ground rules define the field as we use it here.

1. **Advanced aliens only.** We are concerned with life that humanity has at least some chance of encountering within the next hundred years: that is, species able to travel between stars or communicate across them. By this definition humanity itself became "advanced" around the 1950s.
2. **No magic.** Arguments must be bound by the known laws of science in the observable universe. Psychic communication, faster-than-light travel and the like are out of scope. Evolution, as we currently understand it, is assumed to constrain what alien biology and society can be.

The first of these is developed in the [primer](/posts/what-is-astro-sociobiology/).

## Format

Articles here are essays and field notes, not peer-reviewed papers. They are conjectures offered for argument: each states a claim, gives its reasoning, and invites a rebuttal. Articles are grouped into volumes by year, and numbered in order of publication.
